My Life and My Partner's Life

By Davis · Partner: my niece, Omayra

Part 1 · My Daily Routine

  • I wake up at 5:30 a.m.
  • I have breakfast with my family before work.
  • I go to work by car.
  • I eat lunch at noon.
  • I study a postgraduate degree at night.

Part 2 · Omayra's Routine

  • She wakes up at 9:00 a.m.
  • She goes to school by bus.
  • She doesn't eat lunch at home.
  • She watches Korean series in the evening.
  • She eats a lot of pizza, KFC and McDonald's.

Part 3 · Ask and Answer

  • Do you study at night?
    Yes, I do. I study a postgraduate degree.
  • Does Omayra work?
    No, she doesn't. She only studies.
  • What time does she get up?
    She gets up at 9:00 a.m.

Part 4 · Be Ready to Speak

  • Present your work in 2–3 minutes during the next class.
  • Be ready to answer your teacher's questions.

Remember!

  • After does or doesn't, use the base form of the verb.
  • Does she work?
  • She doesn't work.
