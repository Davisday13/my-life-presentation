MY LIFE

Davis & Omayra
👤 Great Idea: Davis
  • I am 43 years old
  • I work as an electrician and a network technician
  • I fix electrical and computer network problems
  • I go out with my family on weekends
  • I like typical food and barbecues
⏰ My Daily Routine
  • I wake up at 5:30 a.m.
  • I have breakfast with my family before work
  • I go to work by car
  • I eat lunch at noon
  • I study a postgraduate degree at night
💬 Ask and Answer
  • Do you study at night? Yes, I do. I study a postgraduate degree.
  • Does Omayra work? No, she doesn't. She only studies.
  • What time does she get up? She gets up at 9:00 a.m.
  • Does Omayra like Korean series? Yes, she does.
👩 Bright Idea: Omayra
  • She is 18 years old (my niece)
  • She studies in high school every day
  • She doesn't work. She only studies
  • She likes to sleep a lot
📺 Omayra's Routine
  • She wakes up at 9:00 a.m.
  • She goes to school by bus
  • She doesn't eat lunch at home
  • She watches Korean series in the evening
  • She eats pizza, KFC and McDonald's
🎯 Creative Tips
  • 3rd person: studies (ESTO-dis), watches (WO-chis)
  • Practice aloud with a timer (2–3 minutes)
  • After does/doesn't the verb has no -s: She doesn't wake up
  • Add details if you have time
