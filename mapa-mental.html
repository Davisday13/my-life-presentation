💬 Ask and Answer Preguntas

  • Do you study at night?
    Yes, I do. I study a postgraduate degree.
  • Does Omayra work?
    No, she doesn't. She only studies.
  • What time does she get up?
    She gets up at 9:00 a.m.
  • Does Omayra like Korean series?
    Yes, she does.

🎯 Tips para hablar Consejos

  • 3ª persona: studies (ESTO-dis), watches (WO-chis), eats, likes
  • Practica en voz alta con cronómetro (2–3 min)
  • Error común: tras does/doesn't el verbo NO lleva -s → She doesn't wake up
  • Si sobra tiempo, agrega detalles: "She studies in the morning"
